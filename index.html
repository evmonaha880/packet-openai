07Essay

Forgetting the Man Who Manufactured Memory

PDF

George Eastman built Kodak and taught the world to remember, then vanished inside his own brand name; the company is now taught only for its collapse. An argument for the century that came first, and a test of how hard AI research can be pushed before it starts inventing things. The essay is the visible half; the other half is the research system it forced me to build: a book-to-knowledge-base extraction pipeline, an image-verification agent, a blind-reader gate, 55 dossiers and an 80-event chronology. That machinery now runs my consulting practice and my job search. Twenty-three pages is what it produced first.
